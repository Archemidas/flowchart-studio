Honest limitation: this is center-channel (mid = (L+R)/2) extraction, not a trained source-separation model. It suppresses hard-panned instruments but does not cleanly separate vocals from drums/bass that are also mixed center — common in hip-hop. If a track sounds worse with this on, turn it off. Transcription no longer depends on it for coverage (see the two-pass pipeline below); it's a minor assist, not the fix.
